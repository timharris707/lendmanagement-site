Privacy policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

Lend Management, LLC operates a small number of private software tools. They are used only by the company's owner, on the owner's own accounts, and are not offered to anyone else.

Google account data

One of these tools signs in to the owner's own Google account to read email and save draft replies on the owner's behalf. It requests only the Gmail permissions needed for that: reading messages and composing drafts. Email it reads stays on the owner's own computers. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising, and it is not used to train AI models.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other information

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking, and does not collect any personal information.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account > Security > Third-party apps.
